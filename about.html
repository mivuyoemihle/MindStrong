About Mind Strong

Learn more about the purpose of Mind Strong and what we aim to provide for men's mental wellbeing.

Our Mission

Mind Strong aims to make men's mental health information easier to understand and access. The website provides educational information and points visitors towards appropriate sources of support.

Our Vision

We want to encourage a culture where men can speak about their mental wellbeing and know that asking for help is an important part of looking after themselves.

What We Provide

  • Mental health information
  • Practical wellbeing resources
  • Information about support options
  • Information about finding professional help

Why Mental Wellbeing Matters

Mental wellbeing can affect how people think, feel and manage everyday challenges. Learning about mental health can help people recognise when they may need support and encourage healthier conversations about wellbeing.
