Support

Information about different types of support that may be available to men experiencing mental health challenges.

Different people may need different types of support. Finding someone trustworthy to talk to can be an important first step when dealing with difficult feelings or challenges.

Counselling and Professional Support

A qualified mental health professional can provide a safe and supportive space to discuss concerns, emotions and everyday difficulties.

Peer Support

Talking with trusted friends, family members or suitable peer-support groups can help people feel connected and supported.

Crisis and Emergency Help

When someone needs urgent assistance, they should contact appropriate local emergency or crisis services as soon as possible.

Supporting Someone You Know

If someone you know is going through a difficult time, listening without judgement and encouraging them to seek appropriate support can be helpful. You do not have to solve their problems yourself.

Important: Mind Strong is an educational website and does not replace professional healthcare. If someone requires urgent help, contact appropriate local emergency or crisis services.
